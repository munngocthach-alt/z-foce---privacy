Delete your data

Z-Force: The Laboratory · developer: the stone · Android app com.thachnguyen.zombieproto

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. This page explains how to delete the data connected to your use of Z-Force: The Laboratory, what is deleted, and what is kept.

How to request deletion

  1. Data on your device (settings, progress, purchase status): uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Z-Force: The Laboratory → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes it immediately.
  2. Advertising data linked to your advertising ID: open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. This breaks the link between you and the data the ad services collected.
  3. Data held for online Co-op and ads (anonymous player ID, connection data, ad data): send an email to us and we will delete it or pass the request to Unity.
    To: gamevui.thestone@gmail.com
    Subject: Z-Force data deletion request
    Include: your device model and roughly when you last played online Co-op.
    You can also ask Unity directly, as described in Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Settings, story progress, purchase status stored on the device Deleted immediately when you uninstall or clear storage.
Anonymous player ID and data linked to it (Unity Gaming Services, online Co-op) Deleted within 30 days of your request.
Advertising data (advertising ID, device information, approximate location from IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics) collected by Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads We forward your request to Unity, which deletes it under its privacy policy. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID also unlinks it from you.
Purchase records for “Remove Ads + Co-op” Kept by Google Play, which processes the payment, as required for tax and legal reasons under Google's Privacy Policy. We cannot delete Google's records. Your purchase can still be restored from Google Play after reinstalling.
Anonymous, aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown) Kept. They contain no information that identifies you.
