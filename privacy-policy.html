Privacy Policy

Z-Force: The Laboratory · developer: the stone · effective 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Z-Force: The Laboratory (“the game”) and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have. The game is developed by the stone (“we”, “us”). Questions: gamevui.thestone@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and the game has no accounts and no chat.
  • The game shows ads (through Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads). Those services collect device information such as your device's advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads.
  • Online Co-op uses Unity Gaming Services to connect you with a friend; it uses an anonymous player ID and your IP address for the connection.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Your settings and progress are stored only on your device.

Information collected

WhatByWhy
Advertising ID, device and operating system information, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions (views, clicks), app version Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) and Unity Ads To show ads when you pause a Solo game, to limit how often they appear, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Ads may be personalised unless you opt out (see “Your choices”).
An anonymous player ID, created on your device; IP address and connection data during an online game Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Relay) To create and join online Co-op games with a join code and to relay the game between the two players. The ID is not linked to your name or email.
Purchase records (order ID, product, purchase time and state) Google Play Billing, read through Unity In-App Purchasing To deliver and restore the one-time “Remove Ads + Co-op” purchase. Payment details stay with Google.
Your settings (controls, sound, graphics), story progress, and whether you own the purchase Stored only on your device So the game remembers them. They are never sent to us.

We do not sell personal information. We do not receive the information the services above collect, except anonymous, aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

Third-party services

These services process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Ads, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Gaming Services): Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google (Google Play, Google Play Billing): Google Privacy Policy

Unity LevelPlay may use other advertising networks to fill ads; information they collect is described in Unity's policy above and in theirs.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: on Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalised ads.
  • No ads: the one-time “Remove Ads + Co-op” purchase turns the ads off.
  • Online play: Solo play does not connect to Unity Gaming Services; only Online Co-op does.
  • Deleting your data: uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device. To ask for the anonymous player ID used for online play to be deleted, email us; see also Unity's policy above for requests to Unity directly.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and information about them was collected, contact us and we will help have it deleted.

Security and retention

Information is sent to the services above over encrypted connections. Information stored on your device stays until you uninstall the game. The services above keep information as described in their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Email us and we will help, or direct the request to the service that holds the information.
